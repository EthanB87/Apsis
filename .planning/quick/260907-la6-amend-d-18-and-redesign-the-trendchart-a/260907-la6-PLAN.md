## Flagged for the user (raised, not silently decided)

The gradient area fill is drawn in **bone** (`#F2F1EC`), the same achromatic family as the ATL line
— it is not a second volt element and does not consume the screen's volt budget. The one real risk
is *luminance* competition rather than *hue* competition: a bone wash under the chart adds a bright
mass to the lower half of the Today screen and could pull the eye away from the HssRing. This plan
caps the top stop at alpha ≤ 0.18 fading to fully transparent, and applies the fill to the **ATL
series only** (not CTL), so the card stays predominantly carbon. If the on-device result still
competes with the ring, lower `AREA_FILL_TOP_ALPHA` — that is the single tuning knob and it is a
named constant for exactly this reason.
