<objective>
TRACER: prove the entire share-card architecture end-to-end on one thin, production-quality
path — the no-photo void card (D-07). Wire ONE entry point (finish screen, D-11) through
every layer this phase will touch: a bone-filled "Share card" button (D-13) opens a
dedicated compose route (D-12), which reads the session's persisted data from SQLite, renders
a 1080x1080 (D-01) void-black card with a share-edition HSS ring (D-02, volt-only / no
readiness band per D-05) and a mono session-type + date caption, then on Share snapshots the
Skia canvas to a PNG, writes it to a cache file, and hands it to the native iOS share sheet.
Sharing is purely user-initiated (D-14).

This is the highest-risk slice in the phase: RESEARCH Open Question 1 flags the
Skia-snapshot -> file -> shareAsync handoff as having zero codebase precedent and requiring
an on-device spike. Proving it first — void card only, no photo picker yet — de-risks the
whole phase before the photo-first flow and full composition are built on top (08-03/08-04).

The card is production-quality, not a prototype: the void card is a REAL shipped feature
(D-07's no-photo fallback), just missing the stat trio, footer branding, and photo background
that the expansion plans add. Functionality gaps (stat trio, photo) are acceptable;
architectural gaps are not — the export pipeline is complete and real here.

Purpose: Validate the render->snapshot->file->share chain end-to-end before expanding.
Output: A working void-card share path from the finish screen, verified on-device.
</objective>
