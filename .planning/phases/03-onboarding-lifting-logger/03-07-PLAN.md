    Create apps/mobile/lib/notifications.ts: wrap expo-notifications — `ensureNotificationPermission()` requests permission on first call (D-26, not during onboarding), returns granted boolean, and tolerates denial gracefully; `scheduleRestNotification(endsAt)` schedules a local notification at endsAt (only if permission granted); `cancelRestNotification(id)` cancels it.
