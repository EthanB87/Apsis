<objective>
Implement the load-trend and readiness halves of the engine: EWMA ATL/CTL/TSB over a dense daily
HSS series (D-10), the pure 3-color `readinessBand` (D-05), and the composite `computeReadiness`
that returns `'calibrating'` until chronic load is established (D-03/D-05). Prove the two pitfalls
that define the product's honesty: a rest week decays load correctly, and a cold-start single
moderate day is `'calibrating'`, never `'red'` (D-04).

Purpose: Readiness is the second pillar of the product (green/amber/red trend). The cold-start
guarantee is an explicit acceptance criterion (BUILD.md §4.4 / RESEARCH Pitfall 2): the app must
not scare a new user with red on day one.
Output: `trend.ts`, `readiness.ts`, and their tests (rest-week decay, band thresholds, cold-start).

Artifacts this plan produces: `computeLoadTrend`, `readinessBand`, `computeReadiness`.
</objective>
